Retract privacy

Last updated: October 2, 2026. Contact: contact@kandalintsev.com.

This page matches the in-app Privacy screen.

We never read your chats. Message text never leaves this phone and is never sent to us.

On this phone

Your Instagram content is processed on your phone. We don’t sell your personal information. We don’t need message text to run cleanup. No analytics SDK. No advertising identifiers. No ads.

Your Instagram login session for this app, chat names / titles, and the date and type of each message you sent stay on this device. Message text is never saved. Local data is encrypted on the device where the platform supports it, and deleted when you tap Disconnect & delete my data (or uninstall).

What leaves this phone

Cleanup talks to Instagram from this phone, the same way you would unsend in a browser. Nothing from your DMs — no message text, photos, or chat participant lists — is sent to a Retract server.

When you connect Instagram, and when you buy Pro (or unlock complimentary Pro with a promo code), Retract emails the publisher your Instagram username, platform, product id if any, and the promo code if you used one. That is only so we know who signed up or subscribed. Purchases themselves are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play; we don’t receive your full payment card number.

Instagram login

You sign in on Instagram’s own page inside the app. We never see your password, never store it, and never send it to a Retract server. Session cookies stay on this device so Retract can continue work you start.

Sample / demo data

Retract may include sample chats for App Review and testing. Sample data is fake, stays on this phone, and never contacts Instagram or a real account. Demo mode does not send signup emails.

What we don’t collect

We do not remotely store Instagram passwords, DM text, inbox contents, photos/videos from your chats, or conversation participant lists. Support email you send us voluntarily may include whatever you write in the message.

Data retention and your choices

Local app data remains on your device until you disconnect and delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall. Ops emails we receive (username / purchase / promo) are kept only as long as needed for support and operations. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in App Store or Google Play settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a store subscription by itself.

Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate for an on-device app. No system is perfectly secure. You are responsible for your device, Apple/Google account, and Instagram account security.

Children

Retract is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child provided information through a support email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Retract isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Instagram or Meta.
