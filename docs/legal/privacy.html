Retract privacy

This page matches the in-app Privacy screen. Contact: contact@kandalintsev.com.

On this phone

Your Instagram content is processed on your phone. We don’t sell or store your messages. We don’t need message content to run cleanup. No analytics. No identifiers sent to us.

Your Instagram login for this app, your chat names, and the date and type of each message you sent stay on this device. Never what the messages say. It’s encrypted on the device and deleted when you tap Disconnect & delete my data.

What leaves this phone

Cleanup talks to Instagram from this phone, the same way you would unsend a message yourself. Nothing from your DMs is sent to a Retract server. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google Play.

Sample data

Retract may include sample chats for App Review and testing. Sample data is fake, stays on this phone, and never contacts Instagram or a real account.

Your password

You type it into Instagram’s own sign-in page. We never see it, we never store it, and it never leaves this phone through us.

Retract isn’t affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Instagram or Meta.
